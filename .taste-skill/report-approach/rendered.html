Time horizons

The tri-horizon research model

Isolating value across decoupled timelines, to limit downside drawdowns and harvest structural market dislocations.

Explore weekly insights

01 / 03

  1. Tactical horizon

    Zero to six months. Tactical liquidity management, currency volatility and systemic liquidity contractions, with bi-directional long/short protocols used to hedge beta and extract upside dispersion.

  2. Fundamental horizon

    Six to twenty-four months. Forensic balance sheet analysis: corporate cash flows restated, leases capitalised and governance screens applied, to filter balance-sheet inflation before capital is deployed.

  3. Structural horizon

    Two to five years and beyond. Sovereign macroeconomic intelligence, debt renegotiation paths and direct unlisted real assets, held through governance and essential infrastructure that compounds across cycles.
