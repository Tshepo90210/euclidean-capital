Institutional foundation

An independent firm, built around one premise

Euclidean Capital was established to build an agile, unencumbered investment house: no distributed consensus committees, no legacy product shelf, and no separation between the firm's own balance sheet and its clients' capital.

In Sub-Saharan African capital markets, policy shifts, currency shocks and sovereign fiscal stress are not externalities. They are fundamental valuation drivers, and our research is built to price them before the market does.

  • Intellectual independence. No distributed consensus committee, and no product shelf to sell.
  • Operational agility. Three people make the decisions, in the time the decision actually takes.
  • Structural integrity. Client capital is held apart from the firm's own balance sheet.
